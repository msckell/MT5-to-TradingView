Two greens, and they never mean the same thing.
The action green is deeper and desaturated; the money green is lighter and brighter; the logo green belongs to the mark alone and never touches the interface. They share a hue family, so the lightness gap is what keeps them apart — never put the two at the same size side by side, and never tint a surface with either. Outside those three colours and the connection dot, the window is grey.
